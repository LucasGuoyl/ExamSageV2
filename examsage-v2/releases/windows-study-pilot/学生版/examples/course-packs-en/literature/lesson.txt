The Window. English translation of an original Chinese teaching poem generated by the ExamSage project on 16 November 2026.
1 The rain has stopped. The window is still open.
2 In the lamplight the empty chair becomes an unsent letter.
3 I put the key back outside the door.
4 The wind gave no answer.Original discussion exercise. One reading sees a wish for contact in the open window and the unsent letter. The key outside makes the speaker's sense of belonging less certain. Another reading sees departure or refusal in the key and the unanswering wind. The open window complicates an interpretation of complete isolation. The poem leaves room for both readings.
